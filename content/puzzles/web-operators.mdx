The starter defines `a` as `7` and `b` as `3`. Add two constants below them:

1. `remainder`, holding `a % b`, the leftover after dividing `a` by `b`.
2. `isBigger`, holding `a > b`, which is `true` only when `a` is greater.

The checks read both values, and also `isBigger && remainder === 1`, which combines them with `&&` and should be `true`.

> [!TIP]
> `%` gives the leftover after division. `7 % 3` is `1`, because 3 goes into 7 twice with 1 left over.
