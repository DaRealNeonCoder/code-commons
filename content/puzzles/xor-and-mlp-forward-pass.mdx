Complete `weighted_sum`, `relu`, and `matvec`. The network is already wired together; your job is to perform its forward pass.

For each row of a matrix, multiply matching positions in the row and input, add the products, and then add that row's bias. Apply ReLU to the two hidden results. The output layer uses weights `[1, -2]`, bias `-0.5`, and sigmoid.

Print `1` when the final probability is greater than `0.5`, otherwise print `0`. The four cases are the XOR truth table. No library is needed.

> [!TIP]
> `matvec` can call `weighted_sum` once per row. The two hidden rows have the same weights but different biases, so the bias is part of what gives them different jobs.
