Style the two text elements on the page:

- The `h1` gets a `font-size` of `48px` and is centered with `text-align: center`.
- The `p` gets a `font-size` of `20px`.

Use element selectors, `h1` and `p`, for both.

> [!TIP]
> `text-align` controls the horizontal position of text inside its box. It doesn't move the box itself. That's a job for the box model and layout lessons.
