The classic. Read an integer `n` and print the numbers from 1 to `n`, one per line, with these replacements:

- multiples of 3 print `Fizz`
- multiples of 5 print `Buzz`
- multiples of both 3 and 5 print `FizzBuzz`

For an input of `5`, a correct solution prints:

```
1
2
Fizz
4
Buzz
```

> [!TIP]
> The order of your checks matters. A number like 15 matches several rules, so test the most specific one first.
