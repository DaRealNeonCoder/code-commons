Complete `hand_derivative(x)` for:

```text
y = exp(sin(x²))
```

The three local slopes are:

```text
dy/d(sin) = exp(sin(x²))
```

Multiply them together. The checker uses a nearby-points estimate and accepts your answer when the two slopes are close.
