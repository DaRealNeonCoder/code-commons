Build a sign-up form with three parts:

1. A `<form>` element that holds everything else.
2. A `<label>` that says `Name`, connected to the input with `for="name"`.
3. A text `<input>` with `id="name"`, `name="name"`, `type="text"`, and `placeholder="Your name"`.
4. A `<button>` with the text `Sign up` and `type="submit"`.

The checks look for each part and read the label and button text.

> [!TIP]
> The `for` on the label must match the `id` on the input exactly. That link is what makes clicking the label move the cursor into the field.
