The starter defines `isRaining` as `true`. Add two pieces of code:

1. An `if` and `else` that sets `plan`, declared with `let plan;`, to `"stay in"` when it's raining and `"go out"` when it isn't.
2. A ternary that sets `umbrella` to `"take it"` when it's raining and `"leave it"` when it isn't.

The checks read `plan` and `umbrella`. Both should match what happens when it's raining.

> [!TIP]
> Try changing `isRaining` to `false` and running it again in your head. Both `plan` and `umbrella` should change to the other choice. The checks only test the raining case, so you can prove the other branch works by reading your code.
