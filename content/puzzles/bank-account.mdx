A bank account can be opened, closed, and used for deposits and withdrawals. Your task is to play through a sequence of operations on one account and report what happens to each.

An account starts out closed. Opening it gives it a balance of 0. While it is open you can deposit money, withdraw money and check the balance; closing it makes it unusable until it is opened again. Operations that are not allowed, such as withdrawing more than the balance, using a negative amount, opening an account that is already open, or touching a closed account, are reported as errors and must not change the account.

## Input

The first line is the number of operations. Each following line is one operation.

## Output

Print one line of result per operation, in order.
