Read a year and print `Leap year` or `Not a leap year`.

A year is a leap year if:

- it is divisible by 4, **except**
- years divisible by 100 are *not* leap years, **unless**
- they are also divisible by 400.

So 2024 and 2000 are leap years, but 1900 and 2023 are not.

> [!TIP]
> You can solve this with `and`/`or` in a single condition, or with `if`/`elif`/`else` checking the rules in the right order.
