The starter code reads a number of seconds. Convert it into hours, minutes and seconds and print them in the format `hours:minutes:seconds`, with no zero padding.

For an input of `3725`, a correct solution prints `1:2:5`, because 3725 seconds is 1 hour, 2 minutes and 5 seconds.

> [!TIP]
> `//` gives whole divisions and `%` gives remainders. Peel off the hours first, then work with what's left.
