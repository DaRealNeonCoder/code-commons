Read an integer `n` and print `prime` if it is a prime number, or `not prime` if it isn't.

A prime number is greater than 1 and divisible only by 1 and itself. So 17 is prime, but 18 isn't (it's divisible by 2, 3, 6, and 9), and 1 is not prime.

> [!TIP]
> Try every possible divisor from 2 up to `n - 1`. If any of them divides `n` evenly, you can `break` out of the loop early. A variable that remembers whether you found a divisor is a handy trick.
