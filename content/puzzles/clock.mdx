Implement a clock that handles times without dates.

You should be able to add and subtract minutes to it.

Two clocks that represent the same time should be equal to each other.

## Input

One line with three integers: the starting hour, the starting minute and the number of minutes to add (negative to subtract). The hour and minute may be out of the usual range.

## Output

Print the resulting time on a 24-hour clock as `HH:MM`, with the clock wrapping around midnight.
