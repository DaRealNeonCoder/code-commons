The starter code reads the three side lengths of a triangle. Print one of these exactly:

- `not a triangle`: the two shorter sides don't add up to *more* than the longest side
- `equilateral`: all three sides are equal
- `isosceles`: exactly two sides are equal
- `scalene`: all sides are different

For `3`, `4`, `5` a correct solution prints `scalene`. For `1`, `2`, `3` it prints `not a triangle`, because 1 + 2 is not greater than 3.

> [!TIP]
> Check for `not a triangle` first. If a side is longer than or equal to the other two combined, there's no point checking the rest.
