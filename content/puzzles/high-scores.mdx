Manage a game player's high score list. Given the scores a player achieved, in the order they were achieved, find the player's three highest scores.

## Input

A single line: the number of scores, followed by the scores in the order they were achieved.

## Output

Print the three highest scores, highest first, separated by spaces. Print all of them if there are fewer than three.
