Implement the encoder as the mean of the row, the decoder by repeating the latent value, and `mse` as the average squared difference between the original row and reconstruction.

This intentionally tiny bottleneck cannot preserve all variation. Its error makes the information trade-off concrete before a learned multi-layer autoencoder adds more capacity.
