In a role-playing game, every character has abilities such as strength, dexterity and constitution. Each ability has a score, and the score determines a modifier that is added to dice rolls.

The modifier is calculated by subtracting 10 from the ability score, dividing by 2 and rounding **down** (towards negative infinity), so a score of 10 or 11 gives a modifier of 0, and a score of 3 gives -4.

## Input

A single integer ability score.

## Output

Print its modifier.
