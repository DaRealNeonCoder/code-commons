Build a card that groups its content together:

- A `<div>` with the class `card`.
- Inside it, an `<h2>` that says `Tip`.
- A paragraph that says `Use semicolons carefully.`, where the word `semicolons` is wrapped in a `<span>` with the class `highlight`.

The span changes nothing about how the words look yet. Lesson 8 explains why it's useful: CSS will style the span in the next part of the course.

> [!TIP]
> Put the `<span>` inside the `<p>`, not around it. A span sits inside a line of text, so it belongs in the paragraph.
