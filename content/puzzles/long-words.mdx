Write a function `long_words(sentence)` that returns every word in a sentence that is **at least 4 characters long**, in their original order. Words are separated by spaces.

For example, `"Please move back to stream"` gives `["Please", "move", "back", "stream"]`; the word `"to"` is too short.

The starter code reads one sentence and prints the matching words separated by spaces. Every test has at least one matching word.
