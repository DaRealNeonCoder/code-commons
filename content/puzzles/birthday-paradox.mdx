The birthday paradox says that in a surprisingly small group of people, it is likely that two of them share a birthday.

A birthdate is a full date of birth (year, month and day); a birthday is only the month and day, which repeat every year. Two birthdates with the same month and day belong to the same birthday.

Your task has two parts:

- Decide whether a list of birthdates contains at least two people who share a birthday.
- Calculate the probability, as a percentage, that at least two people in a group of a given size share a birthday. Assume there are 365 possible birthdays (no leap days) and that every birthday is equally likely.

## Input

The first line is `shared` or `probability`. The second line is a count followed by the values: birthdates like `1999-10-23` for `shared`, or a single group size for `probability`.

## Output

For `shared`, print `true` if two birthdates fall on the same month and day, otherwise `false`. For `probability`, print the chance in percent with exactly six decimal places (for example `50.729723` for a group of 23).
