Build an **XOR** gate from NAND gates: **Q** should light up when exactly one of
the inputs **A** and **B** is on.

Drag NAND gates onto the canvas and wire them between the two switches and the
LED. Click the switches to experiment, then select **Check solution** to test
your circuit on the server.

The labeled inputs and output belong to the puzzle. You can move them, but
cannot delete them. All checker cases are hidden.
