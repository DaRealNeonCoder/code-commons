Build a circuit for this specification: **Q is on when at least two of A, B,
and C are on.**

First write the Boolean expression or truth table on paper. Then use NAND gates
to implement it and test every possible input combination. The fixed inputs and
LED cannot be deleted, but you may place and wire the gates between them.
