Build **AND** from NAND gates: **Q** should light up only when both **A** and
**B** are on.

The first NAND produces `NOT (A AND B)`. Feed that output into both inputs of a
second NAND to invert it again. Drag the NAND gates onto the canvas, wire the
circuit, and choose **Check solution** when it behaves correctly.

The labeled inputs and output belong to the puzzle and cannot be deleted. All
checker cases are hidden so the truth table is yours to reason out.
