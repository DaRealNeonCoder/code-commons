Build a two-bit ripple-carry adder. `A0` and `B0` are the least-significant
bits. The carry from that stage must feed the `CIN` of the next stage.

The checker includes small values, carries between columns, and a final carry
out. You may build the full-adder behavior directly from NAND gates.
