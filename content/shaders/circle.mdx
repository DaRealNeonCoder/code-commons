# **Signed Distance Circle**

This shader turns each pixel into a point centered around the origin. The length of that point is its distance from the center, so comparing it with a radius creates a circle.

`smoothstep` softens the boundary by mapping a small range of distances to a gradual transition. Try changing the radius or the two edge values to see how they affect the shape.
