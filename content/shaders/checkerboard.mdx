# **Animated Checkerboard**

Dividing the screen into twelve tiles per axis gives every pixel a grid coordinate. The sum of the tile coordinates alternates between even and odd, which makes a checkerboard when passed through `mod`.

The pattern is static, but its brightness follows `iTime`. Change the tile count, colors, or pulse speed to experiment with the pattern.
