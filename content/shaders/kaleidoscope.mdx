# **Kaleidoscope**

Polar coordinates make repetition around the center straightforward. `mod` places every angle into one wedge, and `abs` mirrors that wedge so its two sides meet symmetrically.

The folded coordinates drive a traveling wave and a cosine color palette. Change `sectors` to control the symmetry, or adjust the wave frequencies to create different textures.
