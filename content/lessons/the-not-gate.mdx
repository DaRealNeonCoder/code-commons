A **NOT gate** has one input and one output. Its rule is simple:

| Input A | NOT A |
| ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 1 |
| 1 | 0 |

The small circle often drawn at the output of a gate means “invert this.” In everyday terms, a NOT gate can make a warning light turn on when a sensor is *not* active.
