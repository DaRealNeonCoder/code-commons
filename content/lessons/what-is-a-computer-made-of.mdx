You do not need to know how to build a computer before starting this course. We will begin with a simple question: **how can a machine represent a fact and act on it?**

A computer is a collection of parts that work together:

- **Signals** carry changing values, such as a low or high voltage.
- **Bits** give those values a small language: `0` and `1`.
- **Logic gates** transform input bits into output bits.
- **Circuits** combine gates into useful behavior.
- **State** lets a circuit remember something from an earlier moment.

The processor, memory, and devices in a real computer are much larger systems built from these same ideas. This course stays at the level where we can reason about a few inputs and outputs at a time.
