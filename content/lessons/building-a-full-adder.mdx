A **full adder** adds three bits: `A`, `B`, and `CIN` (carry in). It produces `SUM` and `COUT` (carry out).

One beginner-friendly design uses two half adders:

1. Add `A` and `B`.
2. Add that first sum to `CIN`.
3. OR the two carry results.

The circuit is larger, but the idea is still composition. Each stage has a small contract and passes its result to the next stage.
