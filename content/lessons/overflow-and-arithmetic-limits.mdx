A 4-bit unsigned circuit can represent only 0 through 15. If it computes `14 + 5 = 19`, the low four bits contain `0011` and the extra carry is `1`. The hardware did not fail; the chosen width was too small to hold the full answer.

Signed two’s-complement values have a different range. With 4 bits, the usual range is `-8` through `7`. Overflow can happen when adding two positive values produces a negative-looking sign bit, or two negative values produce a positive-looking sign bit.
