To add multi-bit numbers, place one full adder in each bit column. The carry out from the low column becomes the carry in for the next column.

```text
 A1 B1 ──> full adder ──> S1, C2
 A0 B0 ──> full adder ──> S0, C1
                         ↑
                       carry flows upward
```

This is called a **ripple-carry adder** because the carry signal ripples from one stage to the next. It is easy to understand and build, even though faster computers use more advanced carry designs.
