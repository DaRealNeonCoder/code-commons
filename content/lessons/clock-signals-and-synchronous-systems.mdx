A **clock** is a repeating digital signal. We can describe its moments as ticks rather than worrying about the physical oscillator that produces them.

Synchronous systems use a shared clock so that storage elements update at predictable moments. Between ticks, signals can travel through combinational logic. At a chosen edge, registers capture new values. This separation makes a large computer easier to reason about one step at a time.
