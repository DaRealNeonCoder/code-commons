A **D flip-flop** is a one-bit storage element with a data input `D`, a clock, and an output `Q`. On the chosen clock edge, it copies `D` to `Q`. At other times, `Q` holds.

This gives us a clean promise:

```text
Q(next tick) = D(at the edge)
```

The D flip-flop hides the awkward set/reset combination from the previous lesson and is a common building block for registers.
